| `CLOUDFLARE_HYPERDRIVE_TOKEN` | the workspace | BROKERED (`hyperdrive-edit`): applies the Hyperdrive config — the deploy token cannot touch Hyperdrive |

| `SUPABASE_ORG_ID` | the workspace | the Supabase connection's organization id — a fact, not a credential |
| `SUPABASE_PROJECT_REF`, `SUPABASE_DB_PASSWORD`, `SUPABASE_DB_URL` | `next-forge` on stage and on prod | written by the Supabase root's apply: the project's ref, its database password and its connection URL, read by Hyperdrive and the migrations |
| `WIRING_CLOUDFLARE_HYPERDRIVE`, `WIRING_CLOUDFLARE_R2`, `WIRING_CLOUDFLARE_KV` | `next-forge` on stage and on prod | written by the Hyperdrive, R2 and KV roots' applies: the config's id, the bucket's name, the namespace's id — not credentials |
| `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `BASEHUB_TOKEN`, … | `next-forge`, per environment, when the owner stores them | the SaaS keys (README, "Connecting a service"): optional, handed to the Workers as Worker secrets on deploy |

Brokered secrets are minted per run, so no long-lived value is at rest.
The SaaS keys are the product's own, not brokered (above). What the
workspace holds right now (every secret by name, and its integrations and
their health) is on the console's Secrets and Integrations pages for
`org_90386afb433040f9ae8379ff148b8642`.
